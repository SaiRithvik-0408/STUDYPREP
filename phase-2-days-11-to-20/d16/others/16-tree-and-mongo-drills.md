# Day 16: Drills, Quizzes & Practical Exercises

1. **DSA**: In what order does Inorder, Preorder, and Postorder traverse a tree?
   * *Answer*: Inorder: Left $\to$ Root $\to$ Right. Preorder: Root $\to$ Left $\to$ Right. Postorder: Left $\to$ Right $\to$ Root.
2. **Backend**: Why does MongoDB enforce a 16MB maximum document size?
   * *Answer*: To prevent runaway memory bloat during queries and network transfers, and to ensure predictable RAM caching in WiredTiger.
