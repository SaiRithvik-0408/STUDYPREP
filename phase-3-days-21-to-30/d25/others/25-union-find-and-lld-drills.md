# Day 25: Drills, Quizzes & Practical Exercises

1. **DSA**: What is the inverse Ackermann function $\alpha(N)$, and why is Union-Find considered effectively $O(1)$?
   * *Answer*: $\alpha(N)$ grows so extraordinarily slowly that for all practical values of $N$ in the known universe ($N < 10^{80}$), $\alpha(N) < 5$.
2. **Backend**: What is the Open/Closed Principle (OCP) in SOLID?
   * *Answer*: Software entities should be open for extension, but closed for modification. New payment methods should be added by creating new Strategy classes, not modifying existing conditionals.
